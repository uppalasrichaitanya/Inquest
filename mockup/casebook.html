Inquest

Wednesday 21 October, case 7

The Storm at Blackwood

The storm cut the telephone wires at nine. Sir Aldous Blackwood was found at one in the morning, and nobody has gone to bed since. Five guests, five rooms, five weapons. One of them did it.

Each guest had one weapon and was in one room; no two shared. Every statement is true. The killer held the murder weapon in the room where the body was found.
